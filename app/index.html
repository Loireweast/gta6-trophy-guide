    The road to platinum

    Difficulty 4/10 · about 200 hours · one playthrough · no online trophies · cheats switch trophies off · 15 manual saves plus 1 autosave — keep a save at the start and end of every chapter.

    ⚠ One trophy can be missed: Positive Mental Attitude. The Chapter 1 meditation at Jason’s house is gone once Chapter 1 ends. After the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning, follow her out to the balcony when she offers, and accept.
